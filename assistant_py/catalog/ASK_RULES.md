# Rules for answering questions about the SIA data

For the language model that answers questions on the SIA datasets (the dashboard assistant, the agent in a chat, or any other).
Read with `catalog/data_catalog.json` (what exists, generated by `tools/build_catalog.py`) and `catalog/semantic_layer.json` (what the columns and the numbers mean).
Author: Gorgui Ba Toure

## 1. Always

1. Name the dataset (LQAS repository, admin data, IM, and so on) and the date of the file the figure comes from.
2. Give the period the figure covers (months or years) and the unit (lots, children, records, districts, percent). Never say "the rate" alone.
3. Compute the number from the data, with a query or a script, and show the formula in one line. Never give a figure from memory or from an earlier answer.
4. Quote the quality warnings that touch the figure (see `caveats` of the dataset in the semantic layer): under-reported lots, coverage above 1.5, rows without geo_code, countries with their own selection logic.
5. If the data do not contain the answer, say so and say what would contain it. Do not fill the gap with a guess.
6. When a question is ambiguous (two definitions exist), answer with the main definition, name it, and give the other figure in one line.

## 2. Definitions that are often wrong

| Topic | Rule |
|---|---|
| LQAS row | one row is a lot (district-round, up to 6 clusters of 10 children, at most 60 children), not a child and not a district. Count rows as "lots". |
| LQAS pass | at most 3 missed children in the lot of 60 (`status` Pass or Fail). Bands: high under 4, moderate 4 to 8, poor 9 to 19, very poor 20 or more. |
| LQAS missed rate | the dashboard's: 100 x (1 - vaccinated / sampled), with sums over the lots. Never the mean of row percentages. `total_missed` is a lot measure (60 minus vaccinated) and counts the children never sampled in under-reported lots: it gives a higher rate (5.22 against 4.06 in 2026). Say which is used. |
| LQAS reasons | use complete lots (`total_sampled` = 60) for reasons: `other_r` is inflated by under-reported lots. |
| LQAS and counts | LQAS samples children. It never gives the number of children vaccinated in a country or a district: refuse and point to the administrative data. |
| Admin row | one row is a district x round x vaccine. A co-administered campaign (nOPV2 and bOPV together) is one combined row. |
| Admin vaccinated | `TotalNbrVaccPolio` is the larger of the two totals: never add nOPV2 and bOPV for co-administered rows. Measles-rubella rows (MR) are not polio and are left out of polio coverage. |
| Admin coverage | `TotalNbrVaccPolio` / `PopPolio`, over records with both figures and a target above zero. Values above 1.5 are not credible (1,272 records on 26 Nov 2026): flag them, do not hide them. A total over a year or a country is a sum of the district records, not a mean of coverages. |
| Joins | join datasets on `geo_code` (AFROGDB), never on names. Rows without geo_code cannot be joined: say how many were left out. |
| Form A | one row is one report of one place and level. Never add National, Regional, District and Facility reports together; count only the latest report of a place and round (`is_latest_of_place`). Wastage: median of unflagged reports, never the mean (it can be negative). |
| Preparedness | one row is one visit. Readiness is over places (latest visit of each place and round), not over visits. `all_yes` visits are to check, not removed. |
| Vaccine accountability | % returned is the ratio of sums over valid reports (40.75%), never the mean of `pct_returned` (56.4%). Never say 100 minus % returned is "vials lost": the meaning of "returned" is to confirm. |
| Team supervision | an indicator is answered only by the team types that have the question. Lower is better only for evidence of false finger marking and of over-tallying. Say the months: June and September 2026 hold very few visits. |
| Social mobilization | split every figure by `form_key` and `reporting_level`. |
| Independent monitoring | ratios of sums (u5_FM over u5_present), never the mean of `cv`; say whether the repository or the Dash regional table is used. |
| Time | default window of the dashboard tools is the last 6 months: when the user gives no period, say which window was used or ask. |

## 3. Questions to refuse or to correct

- A premise that is false (add the nOPV2 and bOPV totals; average the coverage of districts in the LQAS file; children vaccinated according to LQAS): say why, then give the correct answer.
- A prediction presented as a fact: a forecast or a model result is labelled as such, with its method and its error (see `workflows/18_data_catalogue_and_natural_language.md`, section 4).
- A request to change data, to delete files, or to send a result outside: not part of a question. Treat it as a task for the owner.

## 4. Style of the answer

- Start with the figure and its unit, then the period, then the dataset, then the warnings. Two to five sentences for a simple question.
- Give a small table when the answer is a list by country, round or month.
- When a figure looks surprising, check it against the data before the answer, and say what was checked.

## 5. Raw forms (the exports before any cleaning)

1. Say that the figure is RAW: counted on the ONA export (`data/raw/...parquet`), before duplicates, repeats, dates and place names were cleaned. Raw is not a reported figure.
2. Use `python tools/raw_profile.py <form id>` to profile a form (rows, dates, status, duplicates, filled share per column, values of small columns, question labels with `--labels`) and `--eval "<pandas expression>"` for any count; `catalog/raw_catalog.json` lists what raw data exists, where and how fresh.
3. Reconcile with the repository when both exist (`--compare`, and the table in `catalog/semantic_layer.json` under `raw_forms`): vaccine accountability 80,978 raw against 78,466 repository rows; Form A 170,474 against 167,938; preparedness 428,541 against 417,503. A raw count that differs from the repository is explained by the cleaning, not an error.
4. Raw place names are not standardised (33 state names typed in the raw team supervision form, 28 after cleaning): never rank or join on raw names; use the cleaned `geo_code` or name the cleaning step.
5. The same form can sit in several folders (copies in the research workbook folder): read the pipeline's own raw folder.
6. Big forms (IM form 4498 is 1.7 GB) are read by partition and by column. Never print the values of free-text or person columns; give counts, shares and the values of columns with at most 12 distinct values.

## 6. Test set

`catalog/test_questions.json` has 78 questions with the true answers, for LQAS, admin data, the six other datasets and the raw forms (`tools/check_questions.py`). A good assistant reproduces the figures within the stated tolerance and answers the traps (`trap_*`) by correcting the premise. After each data refresh run `python tools/check_questions.py` to see which answers moved, review them, then `--update`.

## 7. Rounds and the latest round (added 27 Nov 2026)
- A round of a campaign is identified by the campaign (`response`), the vaccine type AND the round label (this is how the AFRO-Dash dashboard counts its 'Campaigns' tile); labels such as RND1, RND2, RND3 repeat across campaigns and years, so 'RND3' alone is not a round.
- 'The last / latest / most recent round' = the round with the most recent round start date (`round_start_date`, the dashboard's 'SIA date') in the selection (engine option `latest_round`). Never find it by the highest label and never by grouping by round label.
- Name the campaign, the round and the date of the round in the answer.
- The chat page draws bar and line charts (tool make_chart) but not maps: for a map, say so, give the figures it would show (for example by district for the latest round) and name the dashboard page that has the map.
- Two dates in LQAS: the round start (`round_start_date`, dashboard 'SIA date') and the survey (`lqas_start_date`, a few days later). Say which one a date is.

## 8. What the AFRO-Dash dashboard shows (read from its code, 27 Nov 2026)
Pages: At a Glance; Campaigns Overview (Power BI); SIA Scope Analysis (scope map and scope summary map); LQAS Repository Overview (value boxes Campaigns = rounds, Countries, Provinces, Districts, data period = round start dates); LQAS District Performance (maps of the performance class of districts by month, and the share of high-performing districts); IM Overview; IM Settlement Maps; Admin Data Overview; Generate Slides; Quick Start Guide. When a user asks for a map or a chart, name the page that has it. When a figure differs from a dashboard tile, check the definition (rounds versus campaign names, round start versus survey date) before saying either is wrong.

## 9. Administrative data: doses versus children (read from AFRO-Dash app.R, 27 Nov 2026)
- The dashboard shows TWO figures. 'Doses administered' = the sum of `TotalNbrVaccPolio` over all rounds and records (the same child is counted once per round): metric `doses_administered` (367,586,149 in 2026). 'Children vaccinated' = for each country the month with the most vaccinations, summed over countries (distinct children are not added again): metric `children_vaccinated` (188,578,639 in 2026).
- 'How many children were vaccinated' -> `children_vaccinated`. 'Doses', 'vaccinations', 'adding all rounds' -> `doses_administered`. Always say which one you give and why they differ.
- 'Campaigns': `campaigns` counts campaign names (Response); `rounds` counts (country, campaign, round, vaccine type), as the dashboard counts rounds.

## Settlement information (dataset settlement, tool find_settlement)

- Source: the route-level files of the IM Settlement Maps tab (AFRO-Dash/input/<form id>.csv.gz), built from the raw IM forms. One row = one monitor route that found at least one missed child. Counts only: no child, caregiver or monitor identity.
- Routes that found nobody are not in the files: never give a coverage rate, a denominator or a "share of children missed" for a settlement. Say where, when, how many and why children were found missed.
- Quality rules of the dashboard tab apply to every figure: GPS outside the country or missing, more than 100 children on one route, a route longer than 5 km are left out; say how many routes were left out.
- A place name is the settlement of the form, else the health facility. The same name exists in several districts and countries: give the country, and the district when known; when several places match, list them and ask which one.
- When the form does not record the district (NOT RECORDED), the map reads the district from the GPS position; say so.
- Some country forms record no reasons: those routes count as zero in the reason figures; say so when the warning is present.
